Beyond Taiwan

Beyond Taiwan（BT）是台灣的學生非營利組織，幫公立高中生看見海外升學的選項。

幹部團隊約 30 人，散在台灣、美國、加拿大、歐洲、澳洲與日韓——大多是自己走過那條路、 或正在路上的學生。

這個網站是什麼

這是 BT 幹部的內部工具，不是對外的服務。它上面目前有一個功能， 另一個在開發中：

  • BT 護照（已上線）——幹部一年有 33 件事要做，散在 11 個月裡。 護照是一個看得到「我做了哪些、還差哪些」的地方，也是留下當時想法的地方。 完成一格蓋一個章。
  • 每週時間看板（開發中）——團隊散在六個時區， 每次開會都要重新約時間。看板讓每個人維護自己的「每週固定有空時段」， 要開會時直接看，不必每次重新問一輪。

誰用得到

目前只有 BT 幹部。註冊需要一組由組長發出的邀請碼——沒有邀請碼的帳號看不到任何內容。

之後會開放給 BT 的學員（高中生）使用。現在還沒有給學員的功能， 但帳號與權限的結構已經先做好了，功能加進來時不需要回頭重做。

資料與隱私

幹部在護照裡寫的心得與上傳的照片只有本人看得到， 這件事由資料庫的權限規則保證，不是靠介面隱藏。BT 的成員包含未滿 18 歲者， 我們把這件事寫得很明確——詳見隱私政策。

本網站不使用任何追蹤或廣告工具。

About Beyond Taiwan

Beyond Taiwan is a student-run non-profit organisation in Taiwan, working to help students at public high schools see their options for studying abroad. The cadre team is around 30 people, spread across Taiwan, the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia, Japan and Korea.

This website is an internal tool for BT cadres, not a public service. It currently hosts one feature, with a second in development:

  • BT Passport (live) — cadres have 33 activities across 11 months of the school year. The passport is where they can see what they have done and what is left, and where they record what they were thinking at the time.
  • Weekly availability board (in development) — the team spans six time zones. The board lets each person maintain their recurring weekly availability, so meetings can be scheduled without asking everyone each time.

Access requires an invite code issued by a team lead. Accounts without one cannot see any content. We plan to open the site to BT's high-school students later; the account and permission structure is already built for that.

What cadres write and the photos they upload are visible only to themselves, enforced by database-level access rules rather than by hiding things in the interface. Some members are under 18, and we are explicit about how their data is handled — see our privacy policy.

This site uses no tracking or advertising tools.
